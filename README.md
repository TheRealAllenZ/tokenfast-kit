## How to use the kit
1. Open your AI app and paste `PROMPT-1.txt` (or upload it). Tap through the audit, then copy your TOKENFAST DATA BLOCK.
2. Open a fresh chat, paste `PROMPT-2.txt`, then paste your block.
3. Answer a few quick follow-ups and get your TokenFast Diet.

Shortcut: upload both prompts in one chat and it runs them in order.
