7-day plan (example)

  1. Day 1: Inbox check to twice a day, with a "skip if nothing new" rule.
  2. Day 2: Close the planning thread. Open a fresh chat with a 5-line brief.
  3. Day 3: Cut standing instructions to one short card.
  4. Day 4: Stop re-uploading the price sheet.
  5. Day 5: Turn web search on only when needed.
  6. Day 6: Share the short card with the part-time helper.
  7. Day 7: 10-minute review: what still hits the limit?
